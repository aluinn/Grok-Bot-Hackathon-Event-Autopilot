# Voice

This folder teaches the tool to write like **your community**, not like a generic robot.

Grok reads these examples before writing copy, so the page copy, newsletter and posts sound like the people who actually run these events.

## What to paste here

Real, past events from your community. For each one, three things:

1. **Event title**: exactly as it was published.
2. **Original description**: the text from the event page (Luma, Meetup, Eventbrite, a flyer, anywhere).
3. **Original post**: one post you wrote to promote it (LinkedIn, WhatsApp or Discord), word for word.

Put them in [`examples.md`](examples.md), one per numbered slot. There are 8 slots. Aim for **at least 5**; more is better.

## How to choose events

- **Pick events you were proud of.** The tool copies what you give it, good and bad.
- **Use your own words.** Don't clean them up or fix typos. Emoji, slang, line breaks and sign-offs are all part of the voice.
- **Mix the types.** A few different events (a talk, a social, a workshop) teach the tool more than five similar ones.
- **Include short and long.** If you sometimes write one line and sometimes three paragraphs, show both.

## What to leave out

- No private details: phone numbers, personal emails, home addresses, or anything an attendee told you in confidence.
- No paid-only or "members only" links.
- No text written by someone else unless they're happy for it to be used.

## What happens next

1. You paste the events into `examples.md`.
2. I read them and write `voice/style.md`: a short guide to how your community sounds (tone, length, favourite phrases, what you never say).
3. Tiago's copy generator uses `examples.md` and `style.md` when it writes for a new event. (`PLAN.md` also mentions loading these into Cognee, Grok Bot's memory. That part is Tiago's.)

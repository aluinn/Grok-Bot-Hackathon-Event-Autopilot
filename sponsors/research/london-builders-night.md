# Sponsor and partner research: London Builders Night — Ship It in One Evening (12 Nov 2026, 18:30, Tech Hub Shoreditch, ~84 makers/designers/founders)

Researched 2026-10-22 by web search; every "Evidence" link was opened on that date, and every email listed is a business address the company itself publishes (partnerships@, events@, community@, hello@, press@, support@ or a contact page) — no personal addresses, nothing from LinkedIn, nothing guessed; where the company publishes no email, the cell is blank and the contact page is the route.
How to use: work top-down by confidence; venue/tooling rows with a programme form (Cloudflare, Grafana, Vercel, JetBrains, ElevenLabs) need 3+ weeks lead time, so send those first; food and drink rows are best asked for product-in-kind for ~84 people; nothing is sent to anyone until the event lead clicks Approve in the Promo and Approval UI.
